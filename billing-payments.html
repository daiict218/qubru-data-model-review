Billing & Payments — Vlocity / FSC (Salesforce source model)

Exact entities and relationships from the Vlocity “Insurance and Financial Services Billing and Payments” ERD (Sept 2020), the money-movement seat (bills issued and payments received). Faithful source model — not yet mapped to Qubru. The view has a small billing core (Payment, Statement) plus the customer-side entities it attaches to (the seam it shares with the Customer Model view).

Validation notes

  • Billing core vs customer seam. Entities 1–2 and relationships 1–8 are the billing-specific objects (new in this view). Entities 3–7 and relationships 9–20 are the customer-side entities billing attaches to — the same objects in the Customer Model view, repeated as the seam.
  • Payment is an External Object — actual payments live in an external system and are surfaced into Salesforce read-mostly via OData / API; that's why it has no __c and sits to one side. It fans out to the statement it answers (#1), what it settles (#2, #3), how it was paid (#4), who paid (#5), and on whose behalf (#6).
  • The Account ↔ Party duality carries over from the Customer Model: holdings (Asset) hang off Party (#9), billing hangs off Account (#12, #14), bridged by "considered as" (#17) / "identified as" (#18). 1:1: #17, #18. Self: Account hierarchy (#16), Asset "provided with" (#20).
  • M:N (1, pink): Person ↔ Account "a contact for" (#19). This billing view draws it as a direct line; the Customer Model view models the same link through its Account Contact Relationship junction entity.

Source: Vlocity “Insurance and Financial Services Billing and Payments” ERD (Sept 2020). Faithful source model — Qubru mapping is a separate step.
